What we do

One team for the whole move

From the first box to the last shelf reassembled - and storage if your dates do not line up.

Home & office moves

Apartments, houses and offices of every size, across Jerusalem and anywhere in Israel. Careful loading, safe transport, everything placed where you want it.

Packing service

Full or partial packing with quality materials - dishes wrapped, wardrobes boxed, every carton labeled so unpacking is easy.

Crane lifts

For the sofa that will not fit the stairs or the piano that will not turn the corner - safe external lifts straight through the window or balcony.

Storage

Clean, organized storage for the gap between homes - a few boxes or a whole household, short or long term.

Disassembly & assembly

Furniture taken apart properly, hardware bagged and labeled, and everything rebuilt solidly at the new place.

Handyman work

Small repairs and odd jobs around the move - shelves, curtain rails, and the little fixes a new home always needs.
