About

The person behind the camera

Har Nof, Jerusalem

Photography that feels like family

I'm Chaim Segal. I photograph the days families talk about for years - Bar Mitzvahs at the Kotel, brit milah mornings, engagements, birthdays and the celebrations in between.

My approach is simple: stay out of the way, stay friendly, and never miss the moment. Most people tell me they barely noticed I was there - until they saw the photographs.

I'm based in Har Nof and photograph events throughout Jerusalem and anywhere in Israel. English and Hebrew spoken.
