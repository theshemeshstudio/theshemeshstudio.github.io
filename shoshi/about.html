About

Art you can hold.

Shoshi creates a wide selection of handcrafted art - stained glass with an emphasis on Judaic themes, and crochet work from rugs to stuffed animals.

Made slowly, on purpose

Every lamp, sun catcher and mezuzah starts as sheets of colored glass, cut and joined by hand. Every blanket and doll starts as yarn and a hook. There are faster ways to make things - this is not one of them, and you can feel the difference.

All items are made in the Shomron, and custom pieces are always welcome: if you can describe it, there's a good chance Shoshi can make it.
