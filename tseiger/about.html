About the Office

A veteran Jerusalem practice.

Meir Tseiger, Adv. & Notary, has practiced real estate and planning law in Jerusalem for decades, serving private clients, families and investors.

The approach

A boutique office in the full sense: the advocate you meet is the advocate who reads every document, appears at every committee, and signs every opinion. Clients get straight answers and fee quotes in writing before work begins.

Languages

Service and documents in Hebrew, English and Arabic - in meetings, in contracts and before the authorities.

Location

Kanfei Nesharim 24, Jerusalem - Givat Shaul, with convenient access and parking.

Practice

Real estate, planning and construction, notarial services, related litigation.

Fees

Written quotes before work begins. Fixed fees for most transaction and notary work.
