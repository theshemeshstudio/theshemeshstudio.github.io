About

Portraits with personality.

The Shuttery is a professional photography service in Jerusalem, run by Raphi Nussbaum, a Canadian-born photographer.

A style that lets people be themselves

Raphi's style is unique: a combination of interacting with your children to help bring out their personalities, while giving them enough space to be themselves. The result is portraiture that feels like your family on its best day - not a stiff afternoon in a studio.

The Shuttery specializes in children's portraits, family portrait sessions and couples, alongside social media and corporate headshots for professionals who want to look like themselves at their best.
