Notary Services

A signature that travels.

As a licensed notary, Adv. Frydman prepares and certifies documents for use in Israel and abroad - with particular experience in German-language legal documents.

For use abroad

  • Notarized translations (German, English, Hebrew)
  • Powers of attorney for use overseas
  • Certified copies of passports and certificates
  • Affidavits and statutory declarations
  • Guidance on apostille and consular requirements

For use in Israel

  • Notarized prenuptial and financial agreements
  • Notarized wills
  • Irrevocable powers of attorney in real estate
  • Certification of signatures and copies
  • Documents for banks, courts and government offices

How it works

Call or write with a description of the document you need. You receive a clear quote and a short list of what to bring. Most notarial acts are completed in a single visit to the office.
