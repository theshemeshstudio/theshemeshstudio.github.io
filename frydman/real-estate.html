Real Estate Law

The largest purchase of your life deserves a careful reader.

Jerusalem property transactions involve unique questions - registration, rights, history. An experienced local lawyer makes the difference between a smooth closing and years of trouble.

For buyers

  • Full rights check before you sign anything
  • Contract drafting and negotiation
  • Purchase tax planning and reporting
  • Registration of rights (Tabu / Land Registry)
  • Second-hand and new-contractor transactions

For sellers

  • Sales contracts that protect you after closing
  • Betterment levy and capital gains questions
  • Mortgage clearance and lien removal
  • Powers of attorney for sellers abroad
  • Inherited property sales
