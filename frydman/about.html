About the Office

One advocate. Your file, personally.

Mendele Frydman, Adv. & Notary, is a boutique law office in the heart of Jerusalem, serving private clients in Israel and abroad.

The approach

Large firms delegate; here, the advocate you meet is the advocate who does the work. Every document is drafted, checked and signed by Adv. Frydman himself. Clients receive straight answers, written fee quotes, and realistic timetables.

Languages

Meetings and documents in Hebrew, English and German. The office has particular experience with German-speaking clients and with documents for use in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Location

Ben Yehuda 34 (Migdal HaIr), central Jerusalem - steps from the light rail.

Clients

Private individuals and families, in Jerusalem and overseas.

Fees

Quoted in writing before work begins. Fixed fees for most notarial and real estate work.
